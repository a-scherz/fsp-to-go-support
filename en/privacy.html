FSP-to-Go Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-11

Who is responsible for the app

FSP-to-Go is operated by FSP-to-Go.

Privacy enquiries: fsptogo.support@icloud.com.

Information the app uses

FSP-to-Go helps you prepare for the Fachsprachprüfung Medizin. You do not need a separate FSP-to-Go account. The app does not ask for your Apple Account password.

Your device stores settings, favourite flashcards, test results and learning progress for medical terms, clinical cases and laboratory values. Results include the time, learning tasks, whether answers were correct and technical record identifiers. The article game also stores its results, counters and frequent mistakes locally. Searches within the learning materials are not sent to a developer-operated server.

The app contains no advertising networks or third-party analytics tools. We do not use this information for advertising, tracking your activity in other apps or selling data.

Clinical cases are part of the app's learning materials. FSP-to-Go is not intended to store your medical records or information about real patients. Do not send such records to support.

iCloud synchronisation

When you are signed into iCloud and allow the app to use it, test history, learning progress and favourite cards can be stored in your private iCloud database and synchronised across your devices. Record identifiers, timestamps and reset or deletion information are used to reconcile changes. The app does not publish this information to other users.

Apple provides storage and transfer. The private database belongs to your iCloud account; its contents are not available to the developer through the CloudKit portal. Apple handles data for its services under its own Privacy Policy.

Without available iCloud, the app stores learning data locally. Delivery of changes between devices depends on connectivity, iCloud settings and service availability. Turning off iCloud does not itself delete data previously stored in the cloud.

Apple Watch and widgets

Apple Watch flashcards use a local copy of learning data. Changes to progress and favourites pass between the watch and paired iPhone through Apple's WatchConnectivity. Random installation and record identifiers, action sequence numbers and change times are also transferred to prevent duplicate processing. These identifiers are not used for advertising or tracking your activity in other apps.

Widgets receive a short progress summary, identifiers of learned materials and the selected language through a shared app container on the same device. This summary does not include test history with answer text.

Retention and deletion

App settings offer separate resets for the article game, favourite cards, clinical-case history, laboratory history and all study history. Each confirmation explains what the action affects. Resetting all study history does not reset the article game.

Changes to synchronised information are delivered to other devices when a connection becomes available. To stop an offline device from restoring removed results, the app may retain technical deletion and reset records, including information about previous results. An in-app reset does not promise immediate physical removal of every copy from iCloud infrastructure or device backups.

You can also manage app data and iCloud backups using Apple's controls. Available actions depend on the system and your Apple Account settings. For help with deletion, use the public contact above.

Support requests

If you choose to send a support request, we receive its contents, your reply contact and any materials you attach. We use them to respond and investigate the issue you describe. Support messages remain in the support mailbox until they are deleted. To request deletion of your correspondence, contact the support address above. Do not send passwords, payment details, patient data or other information that is not needed to resolve the issue.

Your requests and policy changes

For requests concerning access, correction, restriction of processing, deletion or other rights that apply to you, contact the operator using the public contact above. Requests involving your private iCloud may require action on your device or Apple Account because the developer cannot access the database contents.

When the app's practices change, this page will be updated with a new date.
