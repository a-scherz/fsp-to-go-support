FSP-to-Go Support

FSP-to-Go helps you prepare for the Fachsprachprüfung Medizin on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

Contact support

fsptogo.support@icloud.com

Describe the issue and include the app version and build number, device, system version and app language. If possible, include steps to reproduce the problem and a screenshot. Hide personal information before sending it. Do not send Apple Account passwords, payment details or patient data.

Progress from another device is missing

Check that your iPhone, iPad and Mac use the same iCloud account and allow the app to use iCloud. Connect the devices to the internet and open the app on each device. Changes may take time to arrive. Do not delete the app or reset history to fix a delay: doing so may lose local data.

Apple Watch exchanges flashcard progress with its paired iPhone. Check their connection and open the app on both devices. Watch-to-iPhone transfer and iCloud synchronisation between iPhone and Mac are separate steps.

If the problem continues, tell us which data is missing, which device created it and approximately when it changed. The developer cannot access the contents of your private iCloud database.

Resetting data

App settings offer separate resets for the article game, favourite cards, clinical-case history, laboratory history and all study history. Read the confirmation carefully because the actions affect different data. With synchronisation enabled, changes may be delivered to your other devices.

An error in the learning materials

Include the disease or term, language, section and question text. A screenshot helps us find the entry. The materials are for exam preparation and do not replace clinical guidance or advice from a qualified professional.
