FSP-to-Go Support

FSP-to-Go helps foreign-trained doctors prepare German language and professional communication for the Fachsprachprüfung Medizin on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch. It is exclusively an educational app. It must not be used for diagnosis, treatment, research, other clinical activity or decisions about real patients.

Contact support

fsptogo.support@icloud.com

Describe the issue and include the app version and build number, device type, system version and app language. If possible, include steps to reproduce the problem and a screenshot. Hide personal information before sending it. Do not send Apple Account passwords, payment details or patient data.

Report a technical problem

On iPhone, iPad or Mac, open Settings → Diagnostics and feedback → Report a problem. Describe what happened and open Preview technical report to see the technical details. Prepare email opens a draft addressed to support; Share report lets you choose another destination. If email is unavailable, use Share report or contact the address above. Review the content before sending: the app never sends it automatically.

Diagnostic events stay on your device, with at most 50 records from the last 30 days. The report excludes answers and learning progress. You can clear diagnostics or disable local storage on the same settings screen without resetting learning progress. Do not add real patient information to your description. Details are in the privacy policy.

Progress from another device is missing

Check that your iPhone, iPad and Mac use the same iCloud account and allow the app to use iCloud. Connect the devices to the internet and open the app on each device. Changes may take time to arrive. Do not delete the app or reset history to fix a delay: doing so may lose local data.

Apple Watch exchanges flashcard progress with its paired iPhone. Check their connection and open the app on both devices. Watch-to-iPhone transfer and iCloud synchronisation between iPhone and Mac are separate steps.

If the problem continues, tell us which data is missing, which device created it and approximately when it changed. The developer cannot access the contents of your private iCloud database.

Educational-use conditions

The first launch requires explicit acceptance of the educational-use conditions. Without acceptance the learning functions remain unavailable. Acceptance is stored on that device; another device may require its own acceptance. You can read the conditions again in Settings or About. They supplement the Apple standard license agreement and do not restrict mandatory statutory rights.

Sources and errors in learning materials

Medical sources are linked in the app's medical-source screens, including the laboratory reference screen, and in this website's source directory. They help you verify learning material; they do not make the app suitable for clinical use. Exam recollections may contain omissions, and medical information may become outdated. Consult current official sources, clinical guidelines and qualified specialists when questions arise.

To report an error, include the disease, laboratory value or term, language, section and question text. A screenshot helps identify the entry. Support does not provide patient-specific medical advice.

Resetting data

App settings offer separate resets for the article game, favourite cards, clinical-case history, laboratory history and all study history. Read the confirmation carefully because the actions affect different data. With synchronisation enabled, changes may be delivered to your other devices.
