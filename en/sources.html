Laboratory reference values

The values below identify the learning examples and their cited reference methods. They are not universal clinical reference intervals.

Reference intervals depend on the laboratory, method, age and sex. This learning material uses the intervals from the cited sources.

Troponin and CK-MB limits are assay-specific. CK-MB mass is distinct from activity; CK-BB and CK-MM are serum electrophoresis fractions.

BNP uses an acute-heart-failure exclusion threshold. NT-proBNP uses the cited laboratory’s age-specific interval; these values are not interchangeable.

Some entries are decision limits or risk-dependent targets, not universal reference intervals. An eGFR value alone does not establish or exclude kidney disease.

The ESR interval shown applies to adults younger than 50. The source gives different limits at older ages.
