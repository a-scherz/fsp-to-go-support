Educational use · FSP-to-Go

Last updated: 2026-11-12

Purpose

FSP-to-Go is an independent educational app for foreign-trained doctors preparing for the German Fachsprachprüfung Medizin (FSP). Clinical cases, exam situations, questions, terminology and laboratory exercises train language and professional communication. They are not official clinical guidelines and do not train or authorize clinical decision-making.

Permitted use and restrictions

Use the app exclusively for educational FSP language preparation. By accepting, you undertake not to use the app or its materials for diagnosis, treatment, research, or any other clinical activity, nor as a basis for decisions about real patients. FSP-to-Go is not a medical device and provides no patient-specific medical advice.

Accuracy and sources

Medical information can become outdated, incomplete or incorrect. Exam recollections may contain errors or omissions. Laboratory values depend on the laboratory, assay, age, sex and clinical context. When questions arise, consult current official medical sources, clinical guidelines and qualified specialists. Source links in the app support verification of learning material; they do not make the app suitable for clinical use.
